## Summary
Bluetooth devices not autoloaded on reconnect (Ubuntu 20.10 GNOME, Logitech M585 and K380). Service could not inject for unknown device at login time. Fixed in 0.7.0 via udev rule: autoload triggers on device connect, not just at login. Multiple back-and-forth test iterations needed.
