## Summary
Brightness keys (KEY_BRIGHTNESSDOWN/UP) do not report events via evdev on the user's MX Linux/xfce laptop even though the device appears to have the capability. Maintainer confirmed this is an evdev/kernel limitation, not fixable in key-mapper; brightness key events are handled at DE level, not evdev.
