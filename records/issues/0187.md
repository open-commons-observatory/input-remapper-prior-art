## Summary
Logitech G910 G-Keys (G1-G9) report as F1-F9 in evtest because the firmware maps them at hardware level. key-mapper sees F1-F9, cannot distinguish G1 from F1. Only solution is a custom Logitech driver or firmware profile reprogramming. Wontfix in key-mapper.
