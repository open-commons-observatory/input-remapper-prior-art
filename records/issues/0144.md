Kubuntu+KDE: scroll events from key-mapper virtual device don't change scroll focus in apps unless the pane was explicitly clicked first. Works on physical device. Likely a KDE/Qt behavior: focus-follows-hover only for native devices, not virtual uinput. Not reproducible by maintainer on GNOME. Environment incompatibility.
