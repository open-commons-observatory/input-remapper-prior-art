## Summary
PostmarketOS (non-systemd): rc-service could not find key-mapper. Root: key-mapper's D-Bus setup assumes systemd. Workaround: sudo key-mapper-service -d. Also: uinput module not loaded on phone; modprobe uinput fixed injection. Maintainer noted modprobe uinput could be part of service startup.
