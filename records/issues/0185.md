## Summary
GNOME Ubuntu Wayland: "Disable touchpad while typing" stopped working after applying a preset. Root: key-mapper's forwarded keyboard device lacks the input_id properties (vendor, product, bustype) that libinput/GNOME uses to identify it as a real keyboard. Fixed in copy-props branch by copying input_id from the original device to the forwarded uinput. Merged to main.
