## Summary
Holding Shift+mapped combination: after the combo macro runs, subsequent unmapped keys lose the shift modifier. Root: k(Shift_L) in macro sends complete press+release, so shift is down on the "forwarded" device but already released on the "mapped" device. Workaround: end the macro with e(EV_KEY, KEY_LEFTSHIFT, 1) to re-press shift. key_down() would simplify this pattern.
