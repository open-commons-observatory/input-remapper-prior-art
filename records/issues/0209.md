## Summary
Can arbitrary Unicode characters (emoji, coins) be mapped? Maintainer explained: key-mapper sends keycodes, environment decides the symbol; no keycode exists for emoji. Workaround: Ctrl+Shift+U+codepoint (GTK-specific, risky). Docs gap: explicit statement that emoji/arbitrary Unicode is not generally supported.
