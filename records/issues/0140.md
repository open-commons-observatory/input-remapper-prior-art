## Summary
External USB devices freeze in GNOME after key-mapper restarts injection (e.g. on USB hub reconnect). evtest shows events being written correctly; GNOME stops accepting them. Autoload via udev rule may fire too quickly before devices are ready. Workaround: delay autoload by a few seconds. Long-running issue affecting USB KVM users.
