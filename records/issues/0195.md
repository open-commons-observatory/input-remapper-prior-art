## Summary
Cannot map > or $ characters directly. Maintainer explained: KEY_DOLLAR/KEY_GREATER are kernel codes; whether they produce the symbol depends on the keyboard layout in the environment. Solution: use xmodmap-based names (e.g. "less") or macro with Shift_L+key combination. Docs gap: xmodmap vs KEY_ naming distinction.
