## Summary
Scroll events from mapped keys blocked by libinput when mouse cursor is moving simultaneously. Works in Firefox (GTK) but not in GNOME Shell apps (Qt/non-GTK). Root: libinput likely filters scroll events paired with pointer movement as accidental. Workaround: xdotool scroll or wrapping the mapping via xdotool.
