## Summary
Drawing tablet cursor stopped moving when a stylus button was mapped. Tablet (UGEE M708) was mis-detected as a gamepad, so ABS_X/Y events were converted to REL events. Multiple fixes: stricter gamepad detection, ignoring tablet pressure/ABS events, separate forwarding device. Partially resolved; cursor eventually worked after architectural changes.
