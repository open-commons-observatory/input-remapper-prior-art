## Summary
Use "key-mapper keyboard/mouse/gamepad" device names instead of "mapped/forwarded". Design discussion: persistent global uinput devices vs per-injection devices. Tradeoff: persistent devices would allow reusing devices across injection restart (faster switching) but loses per-device property copying.
